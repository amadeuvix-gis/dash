Help & About

This dashboard reads live features from the Rigby feature service and summarizes types in the Layer field. Charts are interactive and will filter the map on click.

Quick tips

  • Use the bar chart to inspect point counts by type.
  • Use the donut chart to inspect area distribution for polygons.
  • Clear filters by double-clicking the right panel area.
